# Data Deletion Instructions

Published as required by the developer policies of Meta (Instagram/Threads), Google, TikTok and X.

## Delete data for one connected account
1. Sign in to Heliograph → **Channels**.
2. Click **Disconnect** next to the account.
3. Access tokens are revoked and deleted immediately; comment/message data for that account is deleted within 30 days; records of published posts (links and statistics) are kept until you delete your Heliograph account, or immediately if you choose "also delete history".

## Delete your entire Heliograph account
1. **Settings → Account → Delete account** (web and mobile).
2. After re-authentication your account enters a 30-day grace period (reversible).
3. After 30 days all personal data, content and tokens are deleted; invoices are kept in anonymised form for the statutory period.

## Requests sent by platforms (Meta Data Deletion Callback)
When you remove Heliograph from your Instagram/Facebook app settings, Meta sends us a signed deletion request. It is processed automatically, the account's data is deleted, and you receive a **confirmation code** with a status page at {{ALAN_ADI}}/privacy/deletion-status?code=….

## Without the app
Email {{EPOSTA_KVKK}} from your registered address with the subject "data deletion"; completed and confirmed within 30 days.
