# Acceptable Use Policy

You may not use Heliograph to:

1. **Fake engagement or spam** — buy or generate followers/likes/comments, run engagement pods, post the same content from many accounts in coordination, stuff irrelevant hashtags, send bulk unsolicited DMs.
2. **Deceive** — impersonate people, clone a real person's voice or face without permission, publish realistic synthetic humans without labels, hide sponsorships.
3. **Circumvent platform rules** — request non-API access, disable automation labels, ask for automated account creation.
4. **Publish illegal or harmful content** — hate speech, harassment, violence, sexual exploitation, fraud, false health or financial claims, copyright infringement, unauthorised personal data.
5. **Connect accounts you are not authorised to manage.**
6. **Attack the Service** — reverse engineer, abuse rate limits, attempt to access other users' data.
7. **Resell** the Service outside the Agency plan.

Enforcement: warning → feature limits → suspension → termination; immediate termination and reporting for serious violations. Report abuse: {{EPOSTA_DESTEK}}.
