# AI Disclosure

Heliograph uses artificial intelligence for content generation and engagement management. Our transparency principles:

1. **Provenance marks.** AI-generated images, audio and video carry machine-readable marks (C2PA/IPTC); provider watermarks (e.g. SynthID) are preserved and cannot be removed.
2. **Platform labels.** Synthetic content that resembles real people or events is published with the platform's AI label (YouTube `containsSyntheticMedia`, TikTok AIGC, Meta AI info). Users cannot disable this.
3. **Sponsorships.** Ad and partnership posts require disclosure tags; ads featuring virtual (AI) characters state that fact, as required by Turkish advertising regulation (amendment effective 1 August 2026).
4. **Replies.** Accounts using the AI reply assistant can disclose that replies are AI-assisted; this is on by default for EU-facing accounts (EU AI Act Art. 50).
5. **Human review.** News-like content and money/legal interactions are never sent automatically; they go to a human approval queue.
6. **Accuracy.** AI output may contain errors; users are responsible for reviewing before publishing. Heliograph provides source citation and fact-check layers but no guarantee.
7. **Data.** Data sent to AI providers is used only to deliver the Service; our contracts prohibit use for model training.
